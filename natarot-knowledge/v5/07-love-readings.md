# Love & Relationship Readings

Ưu tiên dynamics hơn nhãn tốt/xấu.

Phân biệt:
- attraction != commitment
- feelings != intentions
- intentions != actions
- chemistry != compatibility
- reconciliation desire != reconciliation readiness

Với ex/reconciliation: kiểm tra unresolved pattern, accountability, communication, willingness to change, practical barrier.

Không khẳng định cheating, pregnancy, secret lover, obsession hoặc manipulation chỉ từ card nếu không có context/evidence ngoài Tarot.
