# Combination Heuristics v2

Do not store 3,003 pair definitions as authoritative meanings. Generate combinations compositionally.

Signals:
- same suit: domain concentration
- same rank/number: repeated developmental theme
- Major + Minor: archetypal theme expressed through everyday domain
- Court + numbered card: stance/person-role interacting with situation
- upright + reversed of similar themes: expression vs blockage/tension
- ending card with movement archetype: closure may become transition
- emotional cards followed by action cards: feelings move toward behavior
- action cards followed by passive/blocked cards: momentum may meet resistance

Always subordinate heuristics to position semantics.
