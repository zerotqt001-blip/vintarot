# Relationship Deep Reading v3

## Five layers
1. Bond: what actually connects the people?
2. Emotional availability: what can be felt/received?
3. Intention: what direction is desired?
4. Capacity/action: what can realistically be enacted?
5. Pattern trajectory: what happens if nothing changes?

A strong reading may find these layers disagree. Example: Cups can show real feeling while Swords/Wands in action positions show avoidance or inconsistency. Do not flatten that into "they love you" or "they do not."

## Reconciliation
Require evidence across at least three questions:
- Is there emotional residue/desire?
- Is the original obstacle understood or changing?
- Is there capacity for different behavior?

Nostalgia, attraction, loneliness or contact alone are insufficient to conclude sustainable reconciliation.

## Ambiguity
Cards such as Moon, Seven of Cups, Two of Swords, High Priestess or Seven of Swords can indicate incomplete information, projection, guardedness, strategy or uncertainty. Never jump directly to cheating/deception without external evidence.

## Attachment vs compatibility
Devil/strong Wands may intensify attraction; this does not establish compatibility.
Ten of Cups/Lovers may express an ideal or alignment theme; position and surrounding cards decide whether that ideal is lived, desired, blocked or questioned.
